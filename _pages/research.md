My research is in environmental and development economics, with an emphasis on applied microeconomic questions at the intersection of the environment and economic development.

## Research areas

### Climate and agricultural productivity

Research on how heat, water scarcity, adaptation, and production systems shape agricultural productivity.

### Conservation and economic development

Research on the causal effects of protected areas and other conservation policies on local economic outcomes.

### Environment and human capital

Research on how environmental exposures, including heat and air pollution, affect educational and other human-capital outcomes.
