I am an Assistant Professor of Economics and Environmental Studies at Salisbury University. My research lies at the intersection of environmental economics, development economics, and applied microeconomics.

My work examines how environmental conditions and environmental policies affect economic outcomes, with a particular interest in conservation, climate, agriculture, and human capital.

## Research interests

Environmental economics · Development economics · Applied microeconomics

## Current work

My current research includes work on climate and agricultural productivity, the economic effects of conservation policies, and environmental determinants of human capital.
